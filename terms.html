The pages and the film share educational information about a seated wellness practice. They are not a therapy appointment, and they do not replace medical care. Watching the film does not make you a client of the practice.

The films

Nervous System Reset is a guided awareness practice. The sky stays still. You can stop the player whenever you want. The film does not promise a particular feeling, and it does not diagnose or treat a condition.

The player is provided by YouTube. YouTube’s own terms apply when the film plays. A headset or glasses version of the room, when it opens, will be a separate seated session. It is not available on this website yet.
