Privacy

This website does not keep a client record.

Solution Talk VR is an informational site from Solution Therapy & Coaching in Ontario. It explains a seated wellness practice. It is not the confidential file of a therapy appointment.

Updated 15 October 2026.

What this site stores

There is no account, no questionnaire, and no advertising tracker on these pages. The site does not ask for your name, and it does not record where you look, what you say, or what a headset sees.

The pages are published with GitHub Pages. A host of that kind can keep ordinary connection notes, such as an address and the time of a visit, so the site can be served. Solution Talk VR does not receive a list of those visits.

The films

The practice film plays through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player. Pressing play, or opening the film on YouTube, is handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy. The words are already in the picture. This site does not add a second caption file.

Type

The typefaces are requested from Google Fonts when a page opens. That request includes the ordinary technical details a browser sends to load a file.

When you write or call

Email to Hello@SolutionTalkTherapy.com and calls to 437-747-6416 go to the practice. Those messages are not stored on this website. Confidentiality inside psychotherapy is explained in the practice’s informed-consent process. The practice FAQ describes that boundary.

Other sites

Links to solutiontalktherapy.com, YouTube, and Meta leave this site. Those organizations set their own rules once you arrive.
