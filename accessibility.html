Accessibility

The words stay on the page, and in the picture.

Solution Talk VR is meant to be readable without a headset. You can leave any page the same way you arrived.

On these pages

  • A skip link is the first thing a keyboard reaches.
  • Headings follow the order of the page.
  • Links and buttons show a visible outline when focused.
  • The layout keeps working if you enlarge the text.
  • Motion on the site is limited to smooth scrolling, and that scrolling stops when your device asks for less motion.

The film

The spoken lines are written into the sky, under the horizon, so they remain visible in a headset. The player can go full screen. Drag inside the picture to look around. YouTube’s own player controls sit on top of that picture.
