The room meets a setting. It does not decide how you feel.

Seated

You stay in your chair. There is nothing to walk toward, grab, or finish.

Yours to leave

Exit stays on screen. One action stops the scene and brings you back to the room you are actually in.

Softer, not scored

If the moment is too much, Softer turns the sound and the picture down. The session can continue, or you can stop.

Sound

A voice in the room, not inside your head.

Public practices use a quiet spatial ambience and a short invitational script. Alternating tones and binaural beats stay off unless a clinician has set them for you, and they need headphones.

  • AmbienceOn, very low
  • VoiceShort, captioned
  • Bilateral tonesClinician only
  • Binaural beatsOff unless prescribed

Headwear

A headset for the room. Glasses, when they are ready.

You stay seated. The headset version is being built in Unity with the current Meta XR SDKs, aimed at Quest, and at lighter glasses when they ship. Ray-Ban Meta is the audio-only version: the same guided voice, and no picture. Future headwear is coming soon. It joins this room only when it can run that same seated session.

Building for these

A full headset over the eyes. This is the seated path the room is aimed at. A headset that still says Oculus is the earlier Quest line.

Meta VR Glasses

Lighter glasses, with the battery and processor in a puck you keep off your face. Look, then pinch. Meta plans them for spring 2027. They are not on sale yet.

Ray-Ban Meta

The practice without the picture. The guided voice plays through the glasses speakers. This version does not use the camera or the microphone, and it does not open the room. The speakers are open-ear, so people nearby can hear them.
